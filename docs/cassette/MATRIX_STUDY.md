# Matrix — 600 mm coordination study

Matrix is an original OBTP geometric experiment alongside Cassette 01. It does not change the existing Studio, Workshop or Sauna generator. `dist/cassette/matrix.js` reuses the Cassette 01 600 × 2,100 mm wall model, geometry primitives and world-bound transform. New floor/roof tiles and 195 mm corner pieces are **research geometry**, not assessed structural parts or cut files.

`columns × rows` gives the count of 600 × 600 mm planning cells measured between the inner faces of the perimeter walls. Cells do not each become an enclosed room, wall, or separate floor cassette. Straight perimeter segments have one shared 600 mm wall cassette per cell edge; no interior walls are generated. Four distinct 195 × 195 mm corner zones complete the nominal wall depth. Outside wall plywood extends a further 12 mm on every outside face, so the conservative modelled footprint is `(600 × columns + 414) × (600 × rows + 414)` square millimetres. This is a geometric proxy; legal total floor area and site conditions require independent review.

Core floor and roof tiles group up to four columns and two rows: 2,400 × 1,200 mm. A remainder uses the same panel rule in 600 mm increments. Perimeter tiles bridge the 195 mm wall-depth ring, including corner tiles. Each provisional tile has boundary joists, end binders, an intermediate joist at 1,200 mm depth, and an 18 mm deck. Timber dimensions are copied from the nominal Cassette 01 frame for visual comparison only; 2,400 × 1,200 mm sheet fit does not establish a deck grade, span, diaphragm or fastening pattern. The corner study represents four posts and skin returns, without a completed plate, restraint, corner wrap or weatherproofing.

The model includes semantic contact edges with `capacity:null` and `fasteners:null`. They are not engineered fasteners. Tiling across the width introduces a transverse cassette seam that may need a bearer or a designed spanning scheme. The full clear width is therefore the conservative support-spacing screen until a real support layout exists. Studio initially restricts this prototype to 1–8 columns and 1–18 rows, with a 50 m² outside-wall cap; 8 columns correspond to a 4.8 m unbroken clear bearing distance. Roof finishes, foundations, actual structural support locations, columns, connection capacities and weather layers are absent. The Studio selector marks Matrix as **preview only**, not a valid released preset/output, even when the three dimensional screens pass.

Nominal software checks: 4 × 4 gives 7.918596 m², 64 visible full-scene items; 8 × 12 gives 39.699396 m², 156 items and five four-way core tile junctions; 8 × 15 gives 49.084596 m², 182 items. `tests/matrix.cjs` checks part boxes for positive-volume clashes, stage filtering and null interface capacities. No load, vibration, snow, wind, moisture, lifting, joint tolerance, bearing or physical assembly test follows from that result.

Before structural release: compare transverse bearer and continuous-span options; detail the 195 mm corner and perimeter ring, including wall-to-slab and roof uplift paths; resolve four-way cassette junction access and diaphragm forces; specify product properties and site loads; assess the 600 mm wall bay's racking contribution, openings and hold-downs; prototype tolerances and test adjoining assemblies.
